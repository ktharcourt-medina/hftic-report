Who we served

From 2023 to 2026, the HFTICC organization has served a total of 1,905 people. Out of the 1,905 people helped, 454 people are children under the age of 18, 146 people are from the ages of 18-24, 961 people are from the ages of 25-54 and 333 people are from the ages of 55 and above, and all of them are homeless. 180 of the households served have children, and 72.6% of the people served identify as Black or African American.

Out of the 1,905 people who are homeless, 52 people are veterans; the majority of them are adults and seniors from the age range of 35-55 and above. Also, out of the 1,905 people who are homeless, 802 people are chronically homeless. This means a person who has a long-term disabling condition and has experienced long periods of homelessness. 950 people reported a mental health condition.

Program Recidivism

15% of those who exited street outreach to permanent situations within the two-year period leading up to the current fiscal year returned to homelessness within 2 years after their exit. Exits to permanent situations include exits to market-rate and subsidized rental units and staying permanently with family and/or friends.
